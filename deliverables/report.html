2. Motivation

MBA students and operators often face the same evening: a hard academic deadline, a fixed personal obligation, a work ask, and an administrative portal that will not yield without human identity verification. Calendars do not know goal importance. Unconstrained chatbots invent completed phone calls. Task lists do not prepare a private brief and a public calendar invite when a human gate appears.

OUTSOURCE is built as a personal chief-of-staff pattern: understand goals, decide what deserves scarce attention, let AI draft what is draftable, and bring the human in only when required — prepared.

3. What is new here

  • Motion/Reclaim optimize free/busy, not life-goal opportunity cost.
  • General LLMs will claim they “called the insurer” unless hard-stopped.
  • Task managers rarely separate private intervention briefs from public ICS text.

OUTSOURCE’s difference: strategy-aware allocation plus an honest handoff when AI cannot proceed. The insurer path is a labeled simulation, not a fake success.
